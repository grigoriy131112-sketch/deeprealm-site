Art credits
===========

Icons (abilities and monsters) are from the game-icons.net collection,
released under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
license. Each icon is by its original author (e.g. Lorc, Delapouite, Skoll,
Willdabeast, Sbed, Caro Asercion, DarkZaitzev).

  https://game-icons.net/
  License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The icons copied into client/public/art/{abilities,monsters}/ keep their
original filenames' authorship as listed on game-icons.net. If you redistribute
this project, keep this credit file.

No paid or API-generated art is used in this project.

Base map (second licensed raster, deliberate exception)
=======================================================
The world map is drawn on a genuine antique engraving, not on our own art:

  maps/isle-antique.jpg
    Source: Wikimedia Commons, "AMH-7996-KB Map of the island of Boero.jpg"
    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AMH-7996-KB_Map_of_the_island_of_Boero.jpg
    Author: Jakob van der Schley / Pieter de Hondt
    Source collection: Atlas of Mutual Heritage, Koninklijke Bibliotheek
    (Dutch National Library); after 'Oud en Nieuw Oost-Indien' by François Valentyn
    Date: circa 1753
    License: Public domain (published before 1931; faithful reproduction of a
    2-D public-domain work)
    Derived work: downscaled and cropped to a 25:16 window for the web; the
    paper tone is untouched.

  The interactive layer on top of it (roads, seals, labels, party marker) is our
  own vector work. Only the engraving and the parchment texture below are rasters.

Textures (licensed rasters, deliberate exceptions)
==================================================
The world map is otherwise pure vector, but the parchment paper itself is a
single raster texture. It is public domain, so no attribution is legally
required; the credit is kept anyway.

  textures/parchment.jpg
    Source: Wikimedia Commons, "Pergament.1.jpg"
    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pergament.1.jpg
    Author: Membeth
    License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  Derived work: the luminance gradient of the original (one side was much
  darker) was flattened in code, and the tone was normalised to a uniform aged
  paper colour; only the fibre grain remains. Do not replace this with a
  paid or non-free texture.
